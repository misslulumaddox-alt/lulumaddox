A FEW THINGS YOU MIGHT BE WONDERING

Questions? Darling, ask away.

Here are a few helpful details before we get started.

What services do you offer?

Custom digital content, personalized photo and themed content, business advertising and promotional creative, birthday and special-occasion greetings, and ongoing digital girlfriend-style correspondence. Specific deliverables are agreed in advance.

How much does a project cost?

There is no one-size-fits-all price. Pricing depends on the type of project, time involved, requested deliverables, usage rights and schedule. Lulu will provide a quote after discussing your idea.

What is the turnaround time?

Turnaround is determined project by project. The estimated delivery date will be included in the quote or written project agreement before payment.

How do I get started?

Complete the inquiry form with your contact information, budget and project description. Lulu will review it and contact you to arrange a consultation.

What is the digital girlfriend experience?

It is an optional, ongoing digital companionship arrangement that may include agreed daily texts, photos, conversations and regular correspondence. Frequency, boundaries, availability, duration and price are agreed in writing before it begins. It is a digital entertainment service, not an emergency service or a promise of an exclusive real-life relationship.

Can I request adult-themed content?

Adults may inquire about lawful, consensual, age-appropriate adult-themed creative work. All participants must be adults, and no content involving minors, non-consensual imagery or unlawful material will be accepted. Availability depends on the specific request and applicable platform and payment-provider rules.

Will my information and project stay private?

Inquiries and project details should be handled discreetly and used only for responding to and fulfilling the request, subject to the privacy policy and any legal obligations. Do not send highly sensitive information through the inquiry form.

Do you require a deposit?

Deposit requirements, cancellation terms and payment milestones will be stated in the quote before work begins. Do not pay until you have reviewed and accepted those terms.

Can I request changes?

Any included revisions, revision deadlines and fees for additional changes will be stated in the project quote. Please provide clear feedback within the agreed review period.

Do I own the content after I pay?

Not automatically. The quote should specify what rights you receive, whether personal or commercial use is permitted, and whether redistribution, resale, editing or exclusive use is allowed.
