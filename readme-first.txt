LULU MADDOX WEBSITE STARTER
===========================

WHAT'S INCLUDED
- index.html: Welcome / home page
- contact.html: Required project inquiry form + optional details
- payment.html: Payment information page (not yet connected to payment processors)
- faq.html: Frequently asked questions
- thank-you.html: Form submission thank-you page
- privacy.html and terms.html: starter legal pages that must be customized
- styles.css and script.js: site styling and mobile navigation
- images/lulu-hero-placeholder.svg: replace this with Lulu's portrait

IMPORTANT BEFORE LAUNCH
1. Replace images/lulu-hero-placeholder.svg with a portrait named lulu-hero-placeholder.svg, or edit the image src in index.html to your new image filename.
2. The contact form is configured for Netlify Forms. It will only collect submissions after the site is deployed to Netlify with form detection enabled. Test it after publishing.
3. Payment processing is NOT connected. Add a real Stripe/PayPal checkout link or another approved provider only after checking that the provider permits the exact services you sell. Never collect card numbers by email, text or this form.
4. Add your business email, business phone, privacy contact and social links.
5. Customize privacy.html and terms.html with a qualified professional before launch.
6. Set a real adult age-verification process for any age-restricted services; the inquiry checkbox alone is not robust age verification.
7. Consider removing the optional race and sex questions. They are not needed to quote most projects and create extra privacy obligations. If you keep them, explain why you collect them and how long you retain them.
8. Add an explicit project agreement for scope, deliverables, delivery date, revisions, cancellation/refund policy, usage rights, boundaries for ongoing correspondence and communication hours.
9. Keep the digital girlfriend service clearly described as digital entertainment/companionship, with agreed hours and boundaries. Do not promise constant availability or imply a real-life exclusive relationship.
10. Check each payment provider's acceptable-use policy. Some providers restrict adult-oriented content even if it is legal.

QUICK LOCAL PREVIEW
Open index.html in a browser. The contact form won't submit locally.

DOMAIN
The domain is currently registered at Wix. A separate hosting provider is needed if you use this static package. The included site is designed to be hosted as a static site (for example, Netlify). Add the domain to the host first, then update DNS records in Wix exactly as shown by the host. Do not delete Wix nameservers or existing email-related MX/TXT records unless you know what they do.
